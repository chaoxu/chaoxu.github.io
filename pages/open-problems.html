Open problems

These questions remain open to my knowledge. Last status check: 16 November 2026. Solved problems will be removed, and no history of this list will be maintained here.

Problem1Oriented cycle double cover

Does every finite bridgeless multigraph admit a multiset of oriented circuits traversing every edge exactly once in each direction? Parallel edges may form a 2-cycle, whereas traversing one edge forward and immediately back is not a circuit.

See Gáborik et al. (p. 9).

Problem2Deterministic hedge min-cut

Fix an integer k≥2. Let G=(V,E) be a finite connected undirected multigraph with ∣V∣≥2, with E partitioned into nonempty hedges such that the subgraph formed by each hedge and its incident vertices has at most k connected components. Can a minimum-cardinality set of hedges whose removal disconnects G be found in deterministic polynomial time?

Randomized polynomial time is known (Chandrasekaran et al., Theorem 1.1).

Problem3Global edge-bicut

Given a finite digraph G=(V,E) with ∣V∣≥2 and binary-encoded nonnegative rational arc costs, find a minimum-cost arc set F⊆E such that G−F has two distinct vertices with no directed path between them in either direction. Unlike s-t bicut, the separated pair is not prescribed. Is this problem polynomial-time solvable or NP-hard?

Stated as open in Bérczi et al. (p. 413).

Problem4Global linear 3-cut

Given a finite digraph G=(V,E) with ∣V∣≥3 and no prescribed terminals, find a minimum-cardinality arc set F⊆E such that G−F has at least three strongly connected components. Is this problem polynomial-time solvable or NP-hard?

See my thesis (Definition 1.2 and Table 1.2).

Problem5Dichotomy for surjective minimum violation

Fix a finite digraph H=(D,F) with D=∅, allowing loops. Given a finite digraph G=(V,E) with ∣V∣≥∣D∣ and binary-encoded weights w:E→Z≥0​, minimize ∑(u,v)∈E:(ϕ(u),ϕ(v))∈/F​w(u,v) over surjections ϕ:V↠D. Characterize the patterns H for which this problem is polynomial-time solvable, and determine whether it is polynomial-time solvable or NP-hard for every H.

Formulated in Kawarabayashi and Xu (§1, §7), which settles reflexive trees.

Problem6Fast rooted connectivity certificates

Let G=(V,E) be a finite n-vertex, m-arc digraph with root r∈V, and for x∈V∖{r} let λG​(r,x) be the maximum number of arc-disjoint directed r-to-x paths. Given an integer k≥1, can one construct a spanning subgraph H=(V,F) with F⊆E, ∣F∣=O(kn), and λH​(r,x)≥min{k,λG​(r,x)} for all x∈V∖{r} in O(n+km) time, or even O(n+m) time?

Existence of such subgraphs follows from Szeszlér (Theorem 8).

Problem7Minimum submodular k-partition

For every fixed integer k≥6, is there a polynomial pk​ such that, given an arbitrary submodular function f:2U→R via a value oracle on an n-element set U with n≥k, one can partition U into k nonempty parts P1​,…,Pk​ minimizing ∑i=1k​f(Pi​) using at most pk​(n) oracle calls and arithmetic and comparison operations?

For background on fixed-k submodular partitioning, see Hirayama et al. (§7).

Problem8Minimum separated submodular 3-partition

Is there a polynomial p such that, given an arbitrary submodular function f:2U→R via a value oracle on an n-element set U with n≥3 and distinct s,t∈U, one can partition U into three nonempty parts A,B,C with s∈A and t∈B minimizing f(A)+f(B)+f(C) using at most p(n) oracle calls and arithmetic and comparison operations?

Approximation and variable-part variants are studied in Bérczi et al. (§1.1.1).

Problem9Ranked enumeration of submodular 3-partitions

Is there a polynomial p such that, given any submodular function f:2U→R via a value oracle on an n-element set U with n≥3, one can output every unordered partition P of U into three nonempty parts once in nondecreasing order of ∑P∈P​f(P) (ties broken arbitrarily), where producing the first r outputs takes at most (r+1)p(n) oracle calls and arithmetic and comparison operations for each r up to the number of such partitions, and reporting termination takes at most p(n) more?

Ranked enumeration of submodular 2-partitions appears in Hirayama et al. (Lemma 5.2).

Problem10Deterministic Exact Matching

Given a finite undirected graph G=(V,E), a set R⊆E of red edges, and an integer r, is there a deterministic polynomial-time algorithm deciding whether G has a perfect matching M with ∣M∩R∣=r?

Randomized polynomial time is known (see Sebő, p. 6).

Problem11Minimum-cost Exact Matching

Given a finite undirected graph G=(V,E), a set R⊆E of red edges, an integer r, and binary-encoded costs c:E→Z≥0​, can one find a perfect matching M minimizing ∑e∈M​c(e) subject to ∣M∩R∣=r, or report that none exists, in time polynomial in the input bit length (allowing randomized algorithms with success probability at least 2/3 on every input)?

Open even for bipartite graphs (Armbruster et al., §§5.2–5.3).

Problem12Gabow’s serial symmetric exchange conjecture

Let B1​ and B2​ be disjoint bases of a finite rank-r matroid. Can their elements be ordered as B1​={x1​,…,xr​} and B2​={y1​,…,yr​} so that (B1​∖{x1​,…,xi​})∪{y1​,…,yi​} and (B2​∖{y1​,…,yi​})∪{x1​,…,xi​} are both bases for every 1≤i≤r?

Stated in Akrami et al. (Conjecture 1.14) and proved for split and regular matroids.

Problem13Finite-forbidden-set proximity for even delta-matroids

Let (E,D) be a finite even delta-matroid, Γ an abelian group, γ∈ΓE, c∈RE, and F⊆Γ a finite forbidden set. Set λ(X)=∑e∈X​γe​ and c(X)=∑e∈X​ce​, and suppose DF​={X∈D:λ(X)∈/F} is nonempty. For every minimizer A of c over D, must there exist a minimizer B of c over DF​ with symmetric difference ∣A△B∣≤2∣F∣?

Proved for matroid bases in Liu et al. (Theorem 1).

Problem14Labelled-bin filling

Given n bins, m item types (n,m≥1), and binary-encoded integers 1≤w1​<⋯<wm​ with w1​∣⋯∣wm​, targets hi​≥0, supplies Bj​≥0, and preferred counts aij​≥0 (of total input bit length N), find integers xij​≥0 minimizing ∑i=1n​∑j=1m​∣xij​−aij​∣ subject to j=1∑m​wj​xij​i=1∑n​xij​​=hi​≤Bj​​​(1≤i≤n),(1≤j≤m),​ or report infeasibility.

Is this problem solvable in polynomial time for each fixed n≥3 (even when n=3 and wj​=2j−1)? More strongly, is it solvable in g(n)NO(1) time for some computable g, or is the decision version with a binary-encoded cost threshold W[1]-hard parameterized by n? For unrestricted n and m, is there an exact poly(N,wm​)-time algorithm?

See my question on filling bins with minimum moves.

Problem15Covering a square by six unit squares

Can six closed unit squares in the Euclidean plane, translated and rotated independently with overlaps allowed, cover an entire square of side length strictly greater than 2?

Five unit squares cannot, while seven suffice (Dósa et al., Conjecture 1.1 and Theorem 1.3).
