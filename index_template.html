Chao Xu · 许(xǔ)超(chāo)

I'm an Associate Professor at UESTC, a member of the Algorithms and Logic Group. My research interests are algorithms, combinatorial optimization, and computational geometry, along with applied problems that admit clean theoretical structure. Here is my CV.

I maintain a GitHub, an English writing archive (中文博客), a 前沿算法课程 page, and Mastodon @chao. I frequent cstheory and keep a catalog of open problems.

Here are the members of my group. Contact: thechaoxu@gmail.com.
